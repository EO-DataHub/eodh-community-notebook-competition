# eodh-community-notebook-competition
A repository for the EODH community notebook competition

### Data supplied by applicants

Any data supplied by an applicant as part of their submission must be appropriately licensed for public access and use. Applicants must have the necessary rights and permissions to publish the data and must clearly identify its source, ownership and applicable licence. Applicants must not include confidential, personal, restricted or otherwise non-public data unless they have all necessary permissions for its publication and use within a publicly accessible repository. Where third-party open data are used, applicants must comply with the applicable licence conditions, including any attribution requirements.

Commercial, proprietary or otherwise restricted datasets, software, APIs, models or other resources may only be used where their applicable licence terms permit their use in the proposed workflow. Applicants are responsible for ensuring compliance with all relevant licence conditions, including restrictions relating to the **public display, publication or redistribution of source data, intermediate products, derived data, visualisations, model outputs and other derived works**. Where a licence does not permit raw data or derived products to be redistributed, those materials must not be included in the public repository. The submission should instead provide sufficient information for an appropriately licensed user to reproduce the workflow, where the applicable licence permits this.

### Public methods and intellectual property

Competition submissions, including notebooks, source code, documentation and descriptions of methods, are expected to be publicly accessible. Applicants should therefore only submit material that they are comfortable making public. Before submission, applicants should consider whether their notebook contains inventions, methodologies, algorithms, trade secrets, know-how or other intellectual property for which they or their organisation may wish to seek protection. Applicants are responsible for obtaining any necessary approval from their employer, institution, collaborators, funders or other rights holders before submission.

Submission to the competition confirms that the applicant is satisfied that the methods and intellectual property disclosed through their submission may be made publicly accessible under the applicable licences.

It is the applicant's responsibility to ensure that their submission complies with all relevant copyright, intellectual-property, data-protection and licensing requirements. Competition organisers will not assume that the inclusion of material within a submitted notebook establishes that the applicant has permission to publish or license that material.
